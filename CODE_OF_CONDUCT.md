# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Kwartrack a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Focus discussion on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect privacy, especially around personal and financial information.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language and attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `support@kwartrack.com`. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that
violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose
behavior is harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
